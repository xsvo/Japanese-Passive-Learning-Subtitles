# Japanese-Passive-Learning-Subtitles
Turn Japanese subtitles into passive-learning karaoke subs. English meanings in Japanese word order, grammar kept as kana or romaji, optional furigana. Includes auto-alignment, a lyrics mode for songs and built-in flashcards. One click, Windows, offline.

What you need: a video file (.mp4/.mkv) and Japanese subtitles, either inside the video or as a separate .srt/.ass. Subtitles for most shows can be found on [jimaku.cc](https://jimaku.cc/). Works with videos you've ripped from your own Blu-rays or DVDs, bought downloads, or any local file. For best results, play the output in [VLC](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/).

Tip: After each episode, tick the words you've learned under "Words I know". You can also pass them in the flashcards. Then convert the next episode, or rewatch the same one. Known words stay in Japanese, so each conversion shows more Japanese and less English, until you're watching with plain Japanese subtitles.
